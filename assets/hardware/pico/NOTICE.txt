Raspberry Pi Pico R3 — reference geometry by Raspberry Pi Ltd.
Pico-R3-step.zip is the original archive at https://datasheets.raspberrypi.com/pico/Pico-R3-step.zip . The three binary STL files are tessellated from that archive and reoriented; the display colours are assigned by this project. The source assembly is unmodified within the archive.
