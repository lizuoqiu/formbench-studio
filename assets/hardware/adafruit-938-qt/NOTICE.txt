Formwork converted the 29 original components into PCB, display, flexible
ribbon, JST sockets, IC and passive mesh groups. Colors are for visual
identification. The STEP is supplied in its original source coordinate system.
The preview uses millimetres, the PCB mid-plane centre as origin, the glass
facing -Y, and JP2 toward +Z. Board-to-preview transformation from source
coordinates is (17.78 - x, z - 0.785, y - 16.51). Holes are open. The
assembly installation convention places the screen toward the user and the
header along the upper edge. Confirm this orientation when soldering wires.
