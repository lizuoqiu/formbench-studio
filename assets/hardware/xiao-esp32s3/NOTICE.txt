Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32S3, standard board without soldered headers, Sense or Plus expansion:
https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/xiao_esp32s3_getting_started/
Seeed's downloadable original model is XIAO-ESP32S3 v2.step (2023-05-27) in:
https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/SeeedStudio-XIAO-ESP32S3/res/seeed-studio-xiao-esp32s3-3d_model.zip

Nominal board size in the guide is 21 x 17.8 mm. Measured dimensions in this supplier STEP are
20.955 x 17.78 x 1.25 mm for the board envelope and 22.482 x 17.78 x 4.46 mm including the USB-C
port. The standard shield measures 10.6 x 12.6 mm horizontally. Other package sizes and placements
are simplified. Pins, camera, breakout, silkscreen artwork and antenna cable are omitted.
The supplier STEP and its topology, tessellation, textures and images are not included.
Coordinates: PCB centred in XY, USB faces +X, populated side up (+Z), bottom contacts at Z=0.
