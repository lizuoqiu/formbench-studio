Board measurement facts taken from Edge.Cuts, general.thickness, the 2.54-mm-spaced
connector pads and USB0, RST0, BOOT0, ANT0 component centres. The guide gives
a nominal 21 x 17.8 mm format; the PCB source measures 20.955 x 17.78 x 1.6 mm.
Component heights, shield, colour, organic contours, port tongue and switch caps
are approximated for recognition; do not use these to establish enclosure fit.
The detachable antenna and headers are excluded. This is not exact manufacturer CAD.

To regenerate the four groups with ForgeCAD 0.13.0:
forgecad export stl reference.forge.js --param Group=1 --output pcb.stl
forgecad export stl reference.forge.js --param Group=2 --output pads.stl
forgecad export stl reference.forge.js --param Group=3 --output metal.stl
forgecad export stl reference.forge.js --param Group=4 --output polymer.stl
